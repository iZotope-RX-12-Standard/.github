## Audio Restoration Tools

### Noise Reduction

Spectral De-noise helps reduce unwanted noise while retaining useful audio detail. It can be used on voice recordings, music, and other audio affected by steady or changing background interference.

Voice De-noise provides a more speech-focused workflow. Choose settings carefully, as excessive noise reduction can introduce artifacts or make a recording sound unnatural.

### Click and Clipping Repair

De-click targets short impulses and clicks, while De-clip addresses distortion caused by signal levels exceeding the available recording range.

These tools are useful for repairing common recording defects, but severely damaged source material may not be recoverable without audible compromises.

### Dialogue Cleanup

Dialogue Isolate helps separate speech from competing sound in supported workflows. Additional tools can address mouth clicks, plosives, and other vocal recording problems.

The best results depend on the original recording, the level of background interference, and the selected processing settings.

### Spectral Editing

The spectrogram displays frequency content over time, making it possible to identify localized problems that may be difficult to isolate in a waveform.

Spectral Repair and related editing tools can help target specific defects while limiting changes to neighboring audio.

### Music Rebalance

Music Rebalance helps adjust selected elements of a mixed recording, such as vocals, bass, and percussion. This can support remix preparation, balance adjustments, and the creation of alternative mix elements.

Results depend on the source material and the separation process. A finished stereo mix does not provide the same degree of control as the original multitrack session.

## Typical Workflow

1. **Import the recording.** Open the audio file in the standalone editor or use a compatible plugin.
2. **Inspect the source.** Listen to the recording and review the waveform or spectrogram.
3. **Identify the defect.** Determine whether the problem is noise, clipping, clicks, room reflections, or another issue.
4. **Select a suitable module.** Use the processor designed for the specific problem.
5. **Adjust the settings.** Begin with moderate processing and refine the result.
6. **Compare the audio.** Listen to processed and original signals to check for artifacts.
7. **Review the complete recording.** Confirm that the repair preserves important details and sounds consistent.
8. **Export the result.** Select an appropriate output format and audio configuration.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Suggested Check |
|---|---|
| Background noise remains audible | Review the selected module and adjust the reduction settings. |
| Processing sounds unnatural | Reduce processing intensity and compare with the original signal. |
| A plugin is missing | Verify installation, supported plugin format, and host scan settings. |
| Dialogue remains unclear | Check the source recording and experiment with supported dialogue tools. |
| Clicks remain after processing | Refine the selection and use an appropriate repair module. |
| Exported audio sounds different | Review output format, sample rate, and processing settings. |
| Authorization fails | Check the account, license status, and supported activation process. |
